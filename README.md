
# 14-Day Medical ML & Frontier AI Research Plan

1. **Day 1 — Machine Learning Foundations**
   - Supervised learning, features, labels, loss functions, training and generalization.

2. **Day 2 — Generalization, Regularization & Clinical Evaluation**
   - Overfitting, regularization, data leakage, AUROC, AUPRC, sensitivity, specificity and calibration.

3. **Day 3 — Probability, Statistics & Mathematical Foundations**
   - Probability, statistical inference, uncertainty and quantitative reasoning.

4. **Day 4 — Optimization, PyTorch & Representation Learning**
   - Gradient descent, backpropagation, autograd, optimization and learned representations.

5. **Day 5 — Deep Learning & Medical ML Foundations**
   - Neural networks, deep-learning architectures, medical data characteristics and model generalization.

6. **Day 6 — Transformer Mathematics & Optimization**
   - Self-attention, multi-head attention, positional encoding, RoPE, ALiBi, residual connections, LayerNorm, AdamW and gradient optimization.

7. **Day 7 — NLP & Language Modelling**
   - Tokenization, BPE, Word2Vec, GloVe, BERT, GPT, language modelling objectives, perplexity and clinical NLP evaluation.

8. **Day 8 — Computer Vision & Medical Imaging**
   - CNNs, convolution mathematics, MRI, voxels, U-Net, segmentation, transfer learning, Vision Transformers and CLIP.

9. **Day 9 — Multimodal Medical Machine Learning**
   - Early/intermediate/late fusion, cross-attention, missing modalities, MCAR/MAR/MNAR, temporal alignment and multimodal research design.

10. **Day 10 — Advanced LLMs & Medical Foundation Models**
    - Advanced Transformer architectures, KV caching, LoRA, QLoRA, RLHF, DPO, RAG and medical LLM evaluation.

11. **Day 11 — Uncertainty, Calibration, Interpretability & Robustness**
    - Aleatoric and epistemic uncertainty, Brier score, ECE, deep ensembles, conformal prediction, SHAP, Integrated Gradients and Grad-CAM.

12. **Day 12 — Survival Analysis & Rigorous Clinical ML Experiments**
    - Survival functions, hazard functions, Kaplan–Meier, Cox regression, censoring, competing risks and time-dependent evaluation.

13. **Day 13 — Frontier ML Research**
    - Reasoning models, test-time scaling, inference-time compute, agentic AI, multi-agent systems, multimodal agents, world models and automated scientific discovery.

14. **Day 14 — Hands-on Research Capstone**
    - Research problem selection, hypothesis development, methodology, architecture design, PyTorch implementation, experiments, ablations, evaluation and research proposal development.

---

**Overall Goal:** Develop the mathematical understanding, implementation skills and research capabilities to independently investigate modern machine learning, medical AI and frontier AI. And achieve good performance during interviews.
